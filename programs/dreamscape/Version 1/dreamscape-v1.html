Welcome, Architect ✦

A dying star system. Five barren worlds. One long game.

1

Terraform. Build Solar Arrays and Bioreactors to generate Energy and Biomass, then feed a Terraform Station to raise Atmosphere → Water → Biosphere, in that order.

2

Settle. Once air is breathable, raise Habitat Domes to grow population — your people generate Data and Influence for the whole empire.

3

Govern. The Council will periodically ask you to rule on a dilemma inspired by classical statecraft. There is no wrong answer — only a direction for your Dharma Index.

4

Expand. Once a world is reasonably sustainable, launch an expedition to the next of the five elemental worlds — Earth, Water, Air, Fire, Ether.

5

Return. Progress continues while you're away and saves to this browser automatically. No account — just come back and keep ruling.
