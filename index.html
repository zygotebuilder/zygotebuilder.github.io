Welcome to Zygote Builder

A lab of interactive experiments across two universes: Astronomy and Biology. Here is how to explore it.

  1. Start with the flagships. Dreamscape Panchabhuta and the Wellness Bot are the two best places to begin.
  2. Pick an experiment. Use the tabs to filter: Cosmos Lab, Games & Sims, Life & Society, Mind Experiments. Tap a card to launch it.
  3. Switch universes. The ☾/☼ switch flips between Astronomy (dark) and Biology (light). The music changes with it.
  4. Microscope / Telescope. Tap the first tool, then drag the lens anywhere. Dragging aims it at a different patch of sky or slide, so every spot shows something new. Use the mouse wheel or ZOOM (up to 12×) to look closer.
  5. Discover specimens. Centre the lens on a galaxy, cluster, nebula or microbe to learn about it and earn XP. Zoom in to find what is inside: stars within galaxies, mitochondria and DNA within cells. The Codex keeps your collection.
  6. Feeling lost? Press SURPRISE to jump into a random experiment. Press MUSIC to switch sound on or off.
  7. Need this guide again? Tap 📖 MANUAL in the bottom bar, any time.

    About Zygote Builder

    A lab of thought experiments disguised as simulations — built for anyone who looks at the night sky or a drop of water and wonders.

    The mission

    Science feels closest when you can touch it. Zygote Builder turns big questions into small interactive experiments you can zoom into, stack, steer and play with — no textbook, no prerequisites, nothing to sign up for on this page.

    Every experiment lives between two universes: Astronomy — look up, from planets and moons to the edge of the observable universe — and Biology — look closer, from living cells to whole ecosystems. Flip the ☾/☼ switch and the whole lab changes with it. We are quite small… and big as well.

    Who it is for

    Curious people of every age: students, parents, teachers, daydreamers and anyone who enjoys asking “what if?”. If you love science, you are already the target audience.

    The builder

    Zygote Builder is imagined, designed and written by Pradhyumna Nittala, who builds each experiment by hand as a personal lab. The motto is simple: Never finished, always updated. New experiments and improvements arrive continuously, and Zygote is a beginning, not a destination.

    Say hello

    Ideas, corrections, or an experiment you would love to see? Follow along and get in touch through the Newsletter. Also see the Privacy Policy.

    Privacy Policy

    The short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies, and this page does not collect personal information.

    Last updated: 11 November 2026.

    Stored on your device

    To remember your progress, this page saves a few small items in your browser’s own storage (localStorage and sessionStorage): your chosen theme, your XP and rank, the specimens you have discovered, whether you have seen the welcome hint, and a flag so one session is not counted twice. These stay on your device and are never sent to us. You can erase them any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser. Individual experiments may keep their own progress the same way.

    Visit counter

    The “Number of Visits” badge uses a third-party counting service (CountAPI, countapi.mileshilliard.com). It only keeps a running total. Like any web service, it can see technical details of the request, such as your IP address and browser type, under its own policy.

    Fonts

    Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives standard request details (such as your IP address) to deliver them.

    Sound

    Music and effects are generated live in your browser. Nothing is recorded and the microphone is never used.

    Experiments and permissions

    Each experiment is its own page. If one needs a device permission — for example, the camera — your browser asks first and you are free to say no.
