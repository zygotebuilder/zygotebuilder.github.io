PM Modi at SCO Summit: Confront State-Sponsored Terrorism and Double Standards

Direct call made in Bishkek to dismantle global financial networks of terror, emphasizing regional security

Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressing delegates at the 26th SCO Summit.

Addressing the 26th Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Summit in Bishkek, Prime Minister Narendra Modi strongly urged member nations to confront countries using terrorism as an instrument of state policy. In the presence of international leaders, he stressed the absolute necessity to dismantle terror financing, radicalisation ecosystems, and safe havens without exception or regional bias.

Economic Resilience: India's Q1 GDP Touches 7.8%

Robust manufacturing and service sectors defy international financial volatility

Defying global uncertainties and rising crude pressures, India’s real GDP grew by 7.8% in the April–June quarter of FY26-27, outperforming RBI projections. Driven by a 9.2% surge in manufacturing and a robust 10% expansion in services, national economic fundamentals continue to exhibit remarkable strength.

Historic Water Accord Signed

Bihar & Jharkhand resolve decades-old Sone river dispute

In a landmark development in New Delhi, the states of Bihar and Jharkhand signed a formal Memorandum of Understanding for the equitable sharing of Sone River water, witnessed by senior central leaders.

Sports Special: Record-Breaking Feat

15-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi writes history in Duleep Trophy

Young cricketing sensation turns heads on the pitch.

Teenage prodigy Vaibhav Sooryavanshi has etched his name in the record books by becoming the youngest player in the history of the Duleep Trophy to smash a half-century, achieving the milestone in just 42 balls at 15 years of age.
