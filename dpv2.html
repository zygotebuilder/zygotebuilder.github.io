Welcome, Architect ✦

A dying star system. Five elemental worlds. One long game.

1

Look around. In Star Map, tap a planet. On phones, use the 📊 and ⚒ buttons to open your world panels.

2

Build. Solar Arrays make Energy; Bioreactors make Biomass. Tap a building card to buy a level. Then use Terraform Stations.

3

Terraform in order. Atmosphere → Oceans → Biosphere. Habitat Domes need breathable air and water. Population creates Data and Influence.

4

Govern. Council dilemmas now respond to the world you are actually governing. Fire, Water, Air, Earth and Ether create different pressures.

5

Think beyond sustainability. A sustained planet is not finished. Special projects, cities, fleets, research and elemental mastery unlock the long game.

6

Return. Your reign saves in this browser and continues while you are away, with offline progress capped at 8 hours.
