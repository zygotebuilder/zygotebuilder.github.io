PM Modi Renews 'Swadeshi' Pitch, Cites Robust 7.8% Q1 Growth

Prime Minister urges citizens to back domestic economic momentum and practice austerity amidst global uncertainties

Prime Minister Narendra Modi emphasizing self-reliance and domestic economic strength.

Riding on the back of India's strong real GDP growth of 7.8% for the April-June quarter, Prime Minister Narendra Modi has renewed his call for a 'Swadeshi' approach. Urging citizens to focus on domestic potential, support native production, and avoid unnecessary foreign expenditures, the government aims to insulate the national economy from persistent international headwinds.

Supreme Court Relief for NEET Protesters

Apex Court quashes nationwide FIRs, bringing major respite to student groups

In a landmark directive under Article 142, the Supreme Court of India has quashed ongoing FIRs filed against student protesters across the country. The decision follows formal assurances from authorities regarding the peaceful resolution of grievances, leading civil society groups to call off proposed demonstrations in the capital.

Infrastructure Push: 6 New Railway Lines Approved

Ministry of Finance clears major freight corridor expansion under Hybrid Annuity Model

To streamline freight movement and lower logistics costs, the Public Private Partnership Appraisal Committee has greenlit six new strategic railway lines spanning over 647 kilometers, structured through public-private collaboration.

Sports Chronicle: A Teenage Prodigy's Record

15-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi smashes record-breaking Duleep Trophy half-century

Young batting sensation Vaibhav Sooryavanshi rewriting cricketing history.

Displaying phenomenal composure under pressure, 15-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi has stunned the domestic cricket circuit by becoming the youngest player to score a half-century in the Duleep Trophy, taking just 42 balls to reach the milestone.
